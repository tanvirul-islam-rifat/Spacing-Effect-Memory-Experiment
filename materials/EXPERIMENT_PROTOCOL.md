# Experiment protocol

Between-subjects experiment; one session of ~20–25 minutes per participant, run individually by a team member.

## Stimuli — 20 concrete English nouns

Garden · Pencil · River · Basket · Candle · Window · Turtle · Hammer · Pillow · Bridge ·
Bottle · Rabbit · Ladder · Blanket · Whistle · Compass · Anchor · Lantern · Mirror · Feather

Words were chosen to avoid semantic and phonological similarity and to minimise clustering at recall.
The printed participant form and recall sheet are in
[`participant_consent_and_recall_form.pdf`](participant_consent_and_recall_form.pdf).

## Procedure

| Step | Massed group | Spaced group |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Read and sign consent form; told only that this is a "memory study" | same |
| 2 | Condition assigned (see paper for allocation details) and marked on the form | same |
| 3 | Study the 20 words silently in **one 2-minute block** | Study the same list in **three 40-second blocks**, separated by ~1 minute of unrelated arithmetic filler (total exposure = 2 minutes) |
| 4 | **Retention interval:** 10–15 minutes of unrelated filler task (prevents rehearsal) | same |
| 5 | **Free recall:** write down as many words as possible, any order, 2 minutes | same |
| 6 | Sheets scored against the answer key; minor spelling errors accepted if the word is clear | same |
| 7 | Debrief: full explanation of the study's purpose; participant thanked | same |

## Variables

- **Independent variable:** study schedule (massed vs. spaced), manipulated
- **Dependent variable:** number of words correctly recalled (0–20)
- **Held constant:** word list, total study time (2 min), filler task type, retention interval, recall time

## Ethics

Voluntary participation, right to withdraw at any time, signed informed consent, IDs instead of names on recall sheets,
no physical or significant psychological risk. The specific hypothesis was withheld until the debrief to avoid demand
characteristics (minor non-disclosure, not deception).
